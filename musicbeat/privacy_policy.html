RhythmWrist Privacy Policy

Updated: September 8, 2026 | Effective: September 8, 2026

Welcome to RhythmWrist (hereinafter referred to as "this App"). We fully understand the importance of your personal information and handle it with the utmost care in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy will help you understand the following:

  • How we collect and use your personal information
  • How we store and protect your personal information
  • How we share, transfer, and publicly disclose your personal information
  • How you can manage your personal information

Please read and fully understand this Privacy Policy before using this App.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

This App is a music rhythm game that runs entirely offline. It does not collect, upload, or store any personal information of its users.

1. Device Features Used by This App

To provide a normal gaming experience, this App uses the following device features, none of which provide access to any sensitive personal information:

Device Feature Purpose Personal Information Collected
Touch input Recognizing tap, hold, and swipe gestures in the game for note judgment No. Touch coordinates are computed locally in real time only, and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded
Audio playback Playing the background music bundled with the game No. Only built-in music files are played. The microphone is never accessed and no audio is recorded
Screen display Rendering the game scene, notes, and animation effects No

2. Features This App Does Not Use

This App explicitly declares that it does not involve the following features or services:

  • Network access: This App requires no internet connection, sends no network requests, and communicates with no servers
  • User accounts: No registration or login is required, and no identity information such as usernames, passwords, or phone numbers is collected
  • Location: No GPS or network-based location information is obtained
  • Camera/Microphone: The camera is not used and the microphone never records audio
  • Contacts/Messages: Contacts, call logs, and text messages are never read
  • Device identifiers: No device identifiers such as IMEI, OAID, or MAC addresses are obtained
  • Storage access: No personal files, photos, or videos on the device are read or written
  • Background activity: The App pauses when moved to the background and performs no tasks in the background
  • Analytics/Advertising: No third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs are integrated

3. Game Data

Data generated during gameplay, such as scores, combo counts, and grades, exists only temporarily in memory during the current game session and is cleared when the App is closed. No persistent storage of such data takes place.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Personal Information

Since this App collects no personal information, there is no storage, transmission, or cross-border transfer of personal information.

All resources of this App (music files, beatmap data, and image assets) are packaged inside the App and distributed with its installation, without relying on any external network resources.

3. How We Share, Transfer, and Publicly Disclose Your Personal Information

This App collects no personal information, so there is no sharing, transfer, or public disclosure of personal information.

This App integrates no third-party SDKs and shares no data with any third party.

4. How You Can Manage Your Personal Information

Since this App collects no personal information, no personal information management actions are required on your part.

If you uninstall this App, all temporary data within the App is deleted along with it, leaving no residual information on your device.

5. Protection of Minors

This App is a music game suitable for users of all ages. Its content is healthy and positive, containing no violence, pornography, gambling, or other inappropriate material.

This App collects no personal information from minors and requires no parental consent or authorization. Minors can use it with confidence.

6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. When the terms of this Privacy Policy change, we will notify you through a new version of the policy released with an App update.

If you continue to use this App after this Privacy Policy has been updated, you are deemed to have fully understood and agreed to be bound by the revised Privacy Policy.
