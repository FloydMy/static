PRIVACY POLICY

HappyFarm · Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · Effective date: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HappyFarm (hereinafter referred to as "this app"). We take the protection of your personal information and privacy very seriously. This policy explains how this app collects, uses, stores, and protects your information, as well as the rights you are entitled to.

Please read and fully understand this policy before using this app. By tapping "Agree", you confirm that you have read and accepted the entire contents of this policy. If you do not agree, you may tap "Cancel" to exit this app.

2. Information We Collect

This app is a purely offline, single-player app. No account registration is required, and we do not ask for any personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or ID documents.

Data generated while you play (including: virtual coins, land and tree status, poultry status, mission and achievement progress, tool levels, and interface preferences) is local feature data required for the app to run, and is stored only on your device.

This app does not actively collect any sensitive personal information, such as your location, contacts, photo library, or call history.

3. How Information Is Used

The local data described above is used solely to deliver the core features of this app, for example: restoring your farm progress the next time you open the app, settling crop growth and poultry status over time, and remembering your interface preferences.

We do not use this data for any other purpose, do not build user profiles, and do not push advertisements to you.

4. Information Storage and Protection

All data is stored locally on your wearable device using device-local storage and is never uploaded to any server. Since no network transmission is involved, no network leakage can occur.

Local data is saved in the app-specific storage space provided by the operating system, which other apps cannot freely access.

5. Permissions

This app does not request any sensitive permissions such as network, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage. All features work fully offline.

6. Protection of Minors

This app is intended for users of all ages. It contains no social networking, chat, or real-money payment features, and does not collect any information from minors. Minors are advised to use this app with the knowledge and consent of their guardians.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your local data:

  • Clear the app's data in your device's app management settings to delete all local data;
  • Uninstalling this app will also delete all local data;
  • After clearing data or uninstalling, the app will start fresh the next time you open it, without affecting other functions of your device.

8. Updates to This Policy

If app features change in ways that affect this policy, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date accordingly. We recommend that you review this page periodically for the latest information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions regarding this policy or our handling of information, please contact us through the channel where you obtained this app. We will address your feedback as soon as possible.
