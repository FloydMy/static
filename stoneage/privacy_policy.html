StoneDino — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026  |  Effective date: October 5, 2026

This app is an offline casual raising game that runs on your smartwatch. We do not collect, upload, store, or share any of your personal information — all app data stays only on your watch. The app offers optional in-app purchases (see Section 4), which are processed entirely by Huawei's payment service. Feel free to enjoy the game.

1. How We Use Device Capabilities

To deliver its features, the app uses the following device capabilities while you are using it. These capabilities do not read any personally identifiable information:

Capability / Permission Purpose
Accelerometer
(ACCELEROMETER)		 In the "Park", tilting your wrist steers your dino. Sensor data is processed on-device in real time to compute movement and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Vibrate
(VIBRATE)		 Provides light haptic feedback for key in-app events (such as a successful catch or a completed hatch).

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. This app has no account registration or sign-in, and integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only network communication is the system-level Huawei IAP payment flow described in Section 4, which is handled by Huawei services.

The following game progress data is stored only in your watch's local storage so the game resumes where you left off:

  • Your dinos (species, appearance, stage, satiety, growth, etc.);
  • In-game currency and resources (Faith, egg shards);
  • Codex progress, awards, and daily task completion states;
  • App preferences.

3. Storage and Deletion

  • All of the above data is stored on your device only; we keep no copy and have no way to read it;
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local app data, and it cannot be recovered afterwards;
  • You may also clear this app's data at any time in system settings — the effect is the same as uninstalling.

4. In-App Purchases

The Shop offers optional in-app purchases (consumable items and permanent unlocks, priced from US$0.30 to US$1.99). Please note:

  • All payments are processed by Huawei's In-App Purchase (IAP) service through your Huawei account. We never see, collect, or store your payment credentials, billing address, or any financial information;
  • Prices are displayed in your account's local currency during checkout. Purchase records and receipts are kept by Huawei's payment system; we receive only the product identifier of a completed purchase to deliver the item;
  • Permanent (non-consumable) purchases are automatically restored for free when you reinstall the app on any device signed in with the same Huawei account;
  • Purchases are tied to your Huawei account and are subject to Huawei's terms of service and refund policy;
  • All gameplay content can be fully enjoyed without purchasing anything — purchases are entirely optional conveniences.

5. Children's Privacy

This is a casual raising game with family-friendly content: no violence or gore, no chat or social features, and no ads. It is suitable for all ages, including children. Young players are encouraged to play in moderation. Since the app contains in-app purchases, we recommend that parents or guardians supervise purchase activities by minors and set up Huawei account payment protection accordingly.

6. Withdrawing Permissions

You can turn off the accelerometer or vibration permission at any time in your device's "Settings → Apps → StoneDino → Permissions". After that, tilt-to-move and haptic feedback will stop working, but every other feature remains fully usable.

7. Updates to This Policy

If the app's features change in the future (for example, if online features are added), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Any new data collection will be clearly announced and your consent obtained before it begins.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about this policy or how the app handles data, feel free to contact us at floydcash@qq.com. We will respond as soon as possible.

This policy applies to all versions of the StoneDino watch app
