StoneDino — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026  |  Effective date: September 25, 2026

This app is an offline casual raising game that runs on your smartwatch. We do not collect, upload, store, or share any of your personal information — all app data stays only on your watch. Feel free to enjoy the game.

1. How We Use Device Capabilities

To deliver its features, the app uses the following device capabilities while you are using it. These capabilities do not read any personally identifiable information:

Capability / Permission Purpose
Accelerometer
(ACCELEROMETER)		 In the "Park", tilting your wrist steers your dino. Sensor data is processed on-device in real time to compute movement and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Vibrate
(VIBRATE)		 Provides light haptic feedback for key in-app events (such as a successful catch or a completed hatch).

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. This app has no account registration or sign-in, integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests.

The following game progress data is stored only in your watch's local storage so the game resumes where you left off:

  • Your dinos (species, appearance, stage, satiety, growth, etc.);
  • In-game currency and resources (Faith, egg shards);
  • Codex progress, awards, and daily task completion states;
  • App preferences.

3. Storage and Deletion

  • All of the above data is stored on your device only; we keep no copy and have no way to read it;
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local app data, and it cannot be recovered afterwards;
  • You may also clear this app's data at any time in system settings — the effect is the same as uninstalling.

4. Children's Privacy

This is a casual raising game with family-friendly content: no violence or gore, no chat or social features, no ads, and no in-app purchases. It is suitable for all ages, including children. Young players are encouraged to play in moderation.

5. Withdrawing Permissions

You can turn off the accelerometer or vibration permission at any time in your device's "Settings → Apps → StoneDino → Permissions". After that, tilt-to-move and haptic feedback will stop working, but every other feature remains fully usable.

6. Updates to This Policy

If the app's features change in the future (for example, if online features are added), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Any new data collection will be clearly announced and your consent obtained before it begins.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about this policy or how the app handles data, feel free to contact us at floydcash@qq.com. We will respond as soon as possible.

This policy applies to all versions of the StoneDino watch app
